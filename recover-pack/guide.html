The non-negotiable stack

For plant-based recovery, do not chase protein by itself. Pair the repair signal with fuel and fluid.

1

Protein anchor

Tofu, tempeh, beans, lentils, soy yoghurt, seitan, or plant protein.

2

Carb base

Rice, oats, bread, pasta, potato, fruit, wraps, cereal, or noodles.

3

Fluid + salt

Water first. Add salt or electrolytes for sweaty, long, or hot sessions.

If tomorrow feels flat

Do this quick audit before blaming motivation.

P

Protein was missing

Add tofu, tempeh, beans, lentils, soy yoghurt, seitan, or plant protein next time.

C

Carbs were too low

If hunger rebounds fast or training feels heavy tomorrow, increase the carb base.

F

Fluids were ignored

Headache, heavy legs, and weird cravings often mean water and salt were too low.

S

Snack was too small

If the next meal is more than two hours away, make the first recovery feed a real meal.

Save this rule

After training, ask: did I get a protein anchor, a carb base, and fluid? If yes, the recovery window is covered.
