# Shannon Content Edit Memory

Read this before creating or revising Balance feed posts, Stories, Reels, carousels, covers or their automations.

When Shannon gives explicit edit feedback, treat it as a reusable production rule unless he clearly says it applies only once. Record the original direction, translate it into a scalable rule, and update the relevant skill or automation contract. Do not leave important visual or voice preferences trapped inside one preview folder or chat.

## Active production rules

### Real Shannon media first

- Shannon-led coaching and food carousels use approved real Shannon photos when suitable images exist.
- Source from `C:/Users/shann/OneDrive/Desktop/desktop desktop/photos of shannon` before project copies, stock media, generated people or abstract artwork.
- Use different, visibly distinct photos across one carousel. Include both professional and candid images when available.
- Preserve photo provenance in the campaign brief or handoff.

### Every visual must earn its place

- Do not add arbitrary circles, rings, blobs, pills or decorative geometry merely to fill space.
- Shapes are allowed only when they encode a clear relationship, quantity, sequence or brand function that a viewer can understand immediately.
- For food and coaching education, prefer relevant real photography, recognisable food imagery or concise typography over abstract stand-ins.
- If a visual makes the idea stranger or less clear, remove it.

### Shannon's face is protected

- Never place text, captions, cards, diagrams, badges, logos, CTAs or decorative motion over Shannon's face, mouth, eyes, jawline, hairline or head silhouette.
- In graphic-led Reels, use the approved Part Two circular presenter bubble when Shannon needs to remain visible.
- In photo carousels, place copy in genuine negative space using a local translucent field; do not hide most of the photo behind an opaque panel.

### Shannon's spoken delivery

- Use contractions and avoid stiff presenter phrasing.
- Never write the spoken phrase `is not`; use `isn't` or rewrite the thought.
- A normal 45–90 second Reel uses one to three imperfect thinking beats, including at least one genuine `um` or `ah`, plus natural pauses and occasional fragments.
- HeyGen is used for precision lip-sync of an approved reusable Shannon clip, not complete Reel generation.
