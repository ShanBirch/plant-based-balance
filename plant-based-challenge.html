Included in your eight-week challenge

Six weeks of learning. Eight weeks to put it to work.

Balance Learn is included. Its six-week course helps you understand your habits and put practical changes into action. The course sits inside the challenge: learn as you train, then spend the final two weeks making the routine your own.

Weeks 1–6: learn and practise. Work through Balance Learn alongside your workouts, plant-based meals and weekly goals.

Weeks 7–8: keep it going. Continue your training and food routine, revisit what helped and plan what you want to carry forward.

Balance Learn: The Six-Week Course

WEEK 1Why change feels hard⌄

Understand how your brain uses past experience, set your first Weekly Goals, and begin with a workout and meal-plan starting point you can actually use.

WEEK 2Work with your energy⌄

Notice how sleep, stress and low-energy days change your choices. Use the app to keep the next action visible when the week is not ideal.

WEEK 3Build a rhythm that sticks⌄

Use repetition, smaller actions and your surroundings to make training and food habits easier to return to.

WEEK 4Take the fight out of food⌄

Learn what cravings and emotional eating may be trying to solve, then use meal support and food tracking without turning the week into a test.

WEEK 5Make progress easier to repeat⌄

Shape the choices around you, follow your workout plan, and use your real training evidence to see what is working.

WEEK 6Build your sustainable way forward⌄

Bring your training, food and comeback plan together. Finish the course, complete your reflection, and receive your verified Certificate of Completion.

Built by Shannon

Why does change stick for some people, but not others?

I'm an exercise scientist and former gym owner. I've worked with hundreds of clients. Some changed their lives. Others struggled to make it stick. That left me wondering why. Was it really about being lazy or trying harder, or was something deeper going on?

Studying neuroscience led me to look more closely at the habits and automatic responses that shape our everyday choices. I built Balance to help people understand that “autopilot” and practise making changes in their own lives.

The eight-week Plant-Based Challenge brings training, food and accountability together. Balance Learn is included to help you understand the habits behind the work, with practical actions and support from me.

It's more than a meal plan or workout program. It's knowledge you can put to work, with guidance as you practise. The aim is to build skills you can keep using long after these eight weeks.

Clear before you start

Questions about the challenge.

Is the challenge eight weeks or six?

The Plant-Based Challenge runs for eight weeks. Balance Learn is a six-week course included within it. In weeks seven and eight, keep applying what you have learned to your workouts, meals and everyday routine.

Do I have to be vegan?

No. This challenge is for vegetarians and plant-based eaters. We will talk about how you eat, your preferences and your goals so your meal support fits you.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Your training can fit a full gym, dumbbells at home, bodyweight sessions or resistance bands. Balance also includes yoga, recovery and mobility.

What coaching is included, and what does it cost?

Start with a consultation with Shannon. We will talk through your goals, your routine and the support you need, then explain the package, personal review schedule and price before you commit. Live one-to-one workouts are an option to discuss, not a requirement for everyone.

What happens after eight weeks?

Review what has worked and talk through your next steps with Shannon. Any ongoing app access or coaching, its price and terms will be explained separately.
