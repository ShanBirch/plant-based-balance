# Call funnel baseline, 11 October 2026

Read-only production audit, 4 October 00:00 through 11 October approximately 15:12 Brisbane. This is a partial calendar reporting period, not an experiment or unique-person conversion estimate. Sources: bounded reads of ig_messages, ig_threads, growth_outcome_events, balance_bookings, and primary Google Calendar for 4-11 October. Reproduce the aggregate report with scripts/report-lead-call-funnel.mjs; private source-ID evidence belongs in ignored output.

## Findings

- The initial raw unlinked-thread extract contained 624 captured messages across 158 threads. It includes possible client, personal and mirror contamination and is not a count of qualified prospects. The 8 October onward extract contained 288 messages across 85 unlinked threads. These are capture counts, not complete native outreach totals.
- Two Messenger prospects stated clear weight-loss goals on 4 October. One also named time and motivation as blockers. Both first asked for price; the delivered reply offered a phone discussion and asked about goals without answering price. One later received an explicit call invitation and asked for time before continuing. The other ended the captured exchange after stating blockers. These are specific friction examples, not proof that price handling caused silence.
- A 10 October organic exchange moved from studio workload to walking and home weights, then lack of time. The person acknowledged short home sessions could be doable before changing to a product recommendation. This is meaningful training context and a blocker; it does not establish coaching interest or call acceptance.
- Another recent person described wanting more of a particular training format while following a physio's advice about injuries. Preserve the careful response; do not convert that disclosure into commercial readiness. Another enjoyed dance/pole fitness and said the gym is boring; preferences alone are not a coaching need.
- Two Instagram histories contained call-time acceptance or rescheduling. One chose a proposed time and supplied contact details; another requested a reschedule and accepted a new time. These are conversational agreements. Booking and attendance cannot be inferred from reminders or the writer saying a booking was moved.
- There were no booking records created from 4 October through the snapshot. Two confirmed booking rows created on 1-2 October had starts in this week's window; both lacked metadata.ig_thread_id. The calendar contained two campaign consultations this week with matching event IDs. One calendar appointment had been rescheduled while the booking row retained the earlier start. Calendar and database counts therefore cannot be treated as independent booking conversions or joined to lead identities by name.
- No goal, blocker or call-progression events were present in this week's growth outcome trail. The initial all-source event query returned five subscription-started rows, only one linked to an IG thread. They are not five proven new customers from this week's outreach. Paid/trial status and acquisition linkage require separate verification.
- Some saved qualifier evaluations are months old, and recent new threads have none. At least one unlinked thread is receiving coaching check-ins; unlinked does not mean prospect. Native echo author labels also cannot reliably distinguish Shannon from the browser operator.
- The reproducible aggregate query reconciled to 158 active threads, 281 inbound messages and 343 outbound messages. All 158 threads had a missing (104) or pre-period (54) qualifier evaluation. The equal-length previous period had 94 active threads, 514 inbound messages and 320 outbound messages. More captured active threads therefore did not mean more inbound conversation volume. Different source capture, prospect mix and returning conversations prevent treating this comparison as a causal performance result.

## Operating implication

Rapport is working in some exchanges and several conversations have reached useful goals or call logistics. The available evidence does not support a reliable overall goal rate, booking rate, attendance rate, automation ranking or winning phrase yet. The principal verified issue is incomplete progression/booking attribution. The price-answer examples warrant review under the existing direct-answer policy.

Prioritise: source-backed weekly counts and eligibility review; actual booking/calendar reconciliation with attendance unknowns explicit; consistent answers to direct price questions; then one prospective test of moving from training context to the person's desired change. Do not solve a measurement gap by forcing more discovery questions or chasing a declined/unanswered call invitation.

The proposed first test and reusable reporting procedure are in docs/lead-call-measurement.md. No live experiment, message send, booking alteration or operator restart was performed by this audit.
