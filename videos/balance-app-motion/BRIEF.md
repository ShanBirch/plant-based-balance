## Intent

Create a fast, animated vertical ad that shows Balance as a complete fitness app.
The viewer should experience the product as a game plan snapping into place:
plan through weekly step-by-step goals, learn practical fitness concepts, share
meals, walks and workouts, track nutrition, train with Shannon, and compete in
challenges with friends.

## Assets

- Use the real Balance logo and real in-repo app visuals where suitable.

## Customizations

- Six rapid product beats: weekly plan, learning, community, nutrition, coaching, and challenges.
- UI cards stack and transform into the Balance logo for the final lockup.
- No voiceover. Use tactile clicks, swipes, whooshes, and a compact electronic music bed if available.

## Notes

- Vertical 9:16 Instagram Reel.
- 15 seconds.
- Public-facing copy must not mention AI or automation.
- End on "Balance, Fitness Gamified" with a small "Join Balance" call to action.
