# Store submission checklist

## Already prepared

- Bundle/application ID: `com.balance.teleprompter`
- Version: 1.0 (build 1)
- iOS and Android native projects
- Camera and microphone permission descriptions
- Android target SDK 36
- High-resolution Balance app icons and launch artwork
- Three-free-recordings gate and lifetime unlock screen
- Purchase and restore-purchase code through RevenueCat
- Privacy policy and store listing copy
- Phone-friendly Full HD and 4K recording controls

## Requires account access before submission

- Create the app records in App Store Connect and Google Play Console
- Create the non-consumable product `balance_teleprompter_lifetime` in both stores
- Set the Australian price near AUD $9.99 and allow store-localised pricing elsewhere
- Create the non-consumable product `balance_teleprompter_lifetime` in both stores
- Complete Apple Agreements, Tax and Banking and Google merchant setup
- Provide Apple distribution signing and a Google Play upload key
- Upload phone screenshots and complete content-rating, data-safety and app-privacy forms
- If the Google Play account is a newer personal account, complete its required closed test before production access
- Submit each finished release for store review
