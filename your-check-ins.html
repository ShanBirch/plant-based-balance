What Shannon reviews each week

Your weekly check-in helps us understand what's working, what's been tricky and what to adjust for the week ahead.

The full picture

This is not about having a perfect week. Shannon looks at the full picture so your next week can be adjusted around real life.

  • Notice what went well.
  • Keep track as you go.
  • Share what got in the way.

Weekly goals

What felt realistic, what you completed and what got in the way.

  • Pick a few realistic goals.
  • Tick them off as you go.
  • Tell Shannon what got in the way.

Meals and photos

Log your meals and add photos so Shannon can see what the week actually looked like.

  • Log your everyday meals.
  • Add a photo when you can.
  • Mention meals you found hard to plan.

Course progress

What you learned, completed or found difficult in Balance Learn.

  • Work through your current week.
  • Try the action in everyday life.
  • Share anything that felt unclear.

Workouts

Your completed sessions, logged sets and any workouts you had to move or miss.

  • Log the sets and reps you complete.
  • Note sessions you moved or missed.
  • Share what felt too easy or too hard.

Check-in form

Your honest recap of what worked, what did not and what support you need.

  • Share a win from your week.
  • Explain what made things harder.
  • Tell Shannon where you need support.

Progress photos

Your private visual record, used to look for changes beyond one number.

  • Take photos if you feel comfortable.
  • Use similar lighting and angles.
  • Look for changes over time.

Sleep and steps

Your recovery and daily movement, whether entered or brought in from your watch.

  • Log your sleep and daily steps.
  • Check any synced watch entries.
  • Mention changes to your routine.

Mood, energy and stress

The context that helps explain why the rest of your week went the way it did.

  • Notice your mood and energy.
  • Record the stress you felt.
  • Share what affected your week.
