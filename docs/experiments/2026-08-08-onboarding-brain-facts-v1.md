# Onboarding brain facts v1

- Launch date: 2026-08-08
- Variant: `onboarding_brain_facts_v1`
- Decision date: 2026-08-22

## Hypothesis

Short brain and behaviour facts placed beside relevant onboarding questions will make the setup feel broader than nutrition, explain why the questions matter, and help more new members complete onboarding.

## Measurement

- Primary KPI: onboarding completion rate among members who start the setup chat.
- Diagnostics: exposure breadcrumbs for each brain fact, question-level abandonment, and time from onboarding start to completion.
- Guardrails: wizard close rate, median completion time, and support messages indicating that onboarding feels too long or confusing.

## Decision rule

Keep the facts if onboarding completion is stable or improves and the guardrails do not materially worsen. Shorten or remove the weakest facts if completion time or question-level abandonment rises.
