# Balance Learn Instagram Launch

Status: strategy and 14-day feed plan ready for Shannon review. Creative production and publishing remain approval-only.

## Positioning

**Balance Learn: six weeks to understand change, build better habits, and create a way of eating you can maintain.**

Balance Learn is the first step in the wider Balance Path:

1. **Learn**: understand change and build a maintainable base.
2. **Become**: turn repeated actions into evidence of who you are becoming. Coming next.
3. **Master**: build the skill to train, eat and adjust with confidence. Coming next.
4. **Lead**: use lived experience to create a positive influence around you. The existing Balance Identity course is presented publicly as Balance Lead.

Internal course identifiers stay unchanged so member progress, purchases and analytics continue to work.

## Campaign job

Warm the organic audience for 14 consecutive feed days before paid ads begin. The sequence moves from recognition, to mechanism, to proof, to a clear AU$149 offer. Every post has one job and one CTA.

Primary audience: plant-based people who want to train, eat well and feel consistent, but keep restarting when life gets messy.

Core belief: **You are not broken. You do not need more willpower. You need a plan that can adjust when real life happens.**

Primary CTA keyword: `BALANCE`. Keep this keyword because the existing conversation path already recognises it.

## Posting rhythm

- Weekdays: 6:15pm Brisbane time.
- Weekends: 9:00am Brisbane time.
- Use Stories on the same day to reshare the feed post, add one personal context beat, and invite one simple response.
- Do not boost during the 14-day learning period. Use organic saves, shares, profile visits, qualified DMs and landing-page visits to choose the first paid creatives.

## Fourteen-day feed campaign

### Day 1: The promise

- Format: 6-slide photo-first carousel.
- Cover: `YOU DON'T NEED ANOTHER RESTART.`
- Story arc: recognise the restart loop; remove blame; introduce Balance Learn and its six-week promise; show Learn as the first step in a bigger path; close with `Follow the next 14 days.`
- Visuals: photo-free Balance editorial cards in warm cream, black and gold with the large watermark treatment.
- Caption lead: `I kept coming back to the same problem. Most people don't need more information. They need to understand why change falls apart when life gets messy, then practise a way back in.`
- CTA: Follow and save.

### Day 2: A plan for the bad week

- Format: Shannon-led Reel, 35 to 50 seconds.
- Hook: `If your plan only works on a good week, it isn't a very good plan.`
- Reframe: the missed workout is not the failure; having no return plan is the problem.
- Coaching bridge: make the next action smaller, keep one anchor, review what actually happened.
- Proof: Shannon coaching context plus a real Balance Weekly Goals screen.
- CTA: `Comment BALANCE and I'll show you the six-week starting point.`

### Day 3: Motivation is not a system

- Format: 7-slide carousel.
- Cover: `MOTIVATION ISN'T THE MISSING INGREDIENT.`
- Slides: motivation changes; cues and friction matter; shrink the action; keep a visible anchor; review the real week; show how Balance Learn turns this into a weekly rhythm; CTA.
- Caption lead: `Motivation can help you start. It cannot be the only thing holding the week together.`
- CTA: Save for the next flat day.

### Day 4: Show the course, not a feature list

- Format: app-proof Reel, 25 to 40 seconds.
- Hook: `This is what one week inside Balance Learn actually looks like.`
- Visual sequence: week theme; lesson; quiz; practical action; workout or meal support; Weekly Goals; Shannon review.
- Proof source: rebuild from the current live app. Do not reuse a clip that shows Foundations or AU$89.99.
- CTA: `DM BALANCE if you want the full six-week map.`

### Day 5: Eating you can maintain

- Format: 6-slide photo-first food carousel.
- Cover: `THE BEST WAY OF EATING IS ONE YOU CAN KEEP.`
- Slides: reject the perfect meal-plan trap; build around a protein anchor; add useful carbs and plants; make the inconvenient day possible; adjust rather than abandon; connect to Balance Learn.
- Caption lead: `A food plan isn't useful because it looks perfect on Sunday. It's useful when you can still make a decent choice on Wednesday when you're tired.`
- CTA: Share with someone who keeps starting over.

### Day 6: The comeback rule

- Format: Shannon-led Reel, 30 to 45 seconds.
- Hook: `Missing one workout isn't the problem. Turning it into another month is.`
- Reframe: one missed action is data, not identity.
- Action: do the smallest useful version within 24 hours.
- Existing source: adapt the reviewed Comeback belief and coach assets; update every course reference to Balance Learn.
- CTA: `Save this. Your next messy day is exactly when you'll need it.`

### Day 7: Why Shannon built it

- Format: 5-slide founder carousel.
- Cover: `I DIDN'T BUILD ANOTHER SIX-WEEK CHALLENGE.`
- Story: former gym owner and exercise scientist; saw people blame themselves; information was not translating into ordinary weeks; built a course around learning, action and review; Shannon stays in the loop.
- Visuals: mix one professional portrait, one candid photo and one current product screen.
- CTA: Ask a genuine question in the comments.

### Day 8: The complete six-week map

- Format: 8-slide cream-and-gold carousel.
- Cover: `SIX WEEKS. ONE CLEAR JOB EACH WEEK.`
- Slides: Week 1 Why change feels hard; Week 2 Work with your energy; Week 3 Build a rhythm that sticks; Week 4 Take the fight out of food; Week 5 Make progress easier to repeat; Week 6 Build your sustainable way forward; weekly review; offer bridge.
- Guardrail: use the exact live lesson titles during creative production. Do not invent lesson claims.
- CTA: `Comment BALANCE and I'll send the full details.`

### Day 9: Proof of adjustment

- Format: 6 to 8-slide client-safe proof carousel.
- Cover: `THE RESULT WASN'T A PERFECT WEEK.`
- Arc: specific starting problem; the real receipt; what changed; what Shannon noticed; the adjustment; what the proof does and does not show.
- Source: use a client story only with confirmed public consent. Otherwise use Shannon's own logged session or anonymised object-only app proof.
- CTA: `This is what course correction looks like. Save it.`

### Day 10: What the weekly review means

- Format: Shannon-led Reel, 35 to 50 seconds.
- Hook: `Weekly coaching isn't me asking whether you were good or bad.`
- Mechanism: look at training, food, the plan and the life around it; find the useful pattern; change one thing for the next week.
- Proof: current check-in and plan-review screens, with no private client details.
- CTA: `DM BALANCE if this is the bit you've been missing.`

### Day 11: Who it is and is not for

- Format: 7-slide carousel.
- Cover: `BALANCE LEARN ISN'T FOR EVERYONE.`
- For: people tired of restarting; willing to use the app, lessons and check-in; want training and eating to fit real life.
- Not for: instant replies; clinical treatment; passive PDF collecting; guaranteed outcomes without participation.
- Offer truth: one AUD $149 payment, six weeks, no subscription or automatic renewal.
- CTA: Send it to someone deciding whether it fits.

### Day 12: The wider Balance Path

- Format: product-vision Reel, 25 to 40 seconds.
- Hook: `Balance Learn is the start. It isn't the whole path.`
- Sequence: Learn; Become, coming next; Master, coming next; Lead, the existing advanced social-influence course.
- Promise: give the audience a clear direction without selling unfinished courses.
- CTA: `Which stage sounds most like where you are now?`

### Day 13: Everything included

- Format: 7-slide offer carousel.
- Cover: `WHAT YOUR AU$149 ACTUALLY INCLUDES.`
- Slides: complete six-week Balance Learn course; six weeks of app and community access; workout structure; meal-plan support; Weekly Goals; one weekly check-in plus workout and food review with Shannon; no subscription or automatic renewal.
- Visuals: current product screens dominate the feature slides. Avoid generic icon cards.
- CTA: `Comment BALANCE and I'll send you the link.`

### Day 14: Launch invitation

- Format: Shannon-led launch Reel, 30 to 45 seconds.
- Hook: `If you're tired of knowing what to do and still restarting, this is for you.`
- Arc: recognise; remove blame; give the six-week promise; show the weekly course rhythm; name Shannon's review; state AU$149 once; invite the next step.
- Closing line: `Don't restart again. Learn how to get back in Balance.`
- CTA: `Comment or DM BALANCE, or use the link in my bio.`

## Stories that support each feed post

Use three simple frames rather than creating a second full campaign every day:

1. Real-life context from Shannon: what made today's idea worth posting.
2. Reshare the feed post with one line telling people what they will get from it.
3. One interaction: poll, question sticker, slider or `DM BALANCE` invitation.
