How cancellation works

Direct recurring subscriptions purchased from Balance on or after 1 September 2026 require 30 days' notice. Billing and access continue during that period. If your coaching plan still has an initial minimum, the later date applies.

Subscriptions purchased earlier keep the terms accepted at purchase. Apple and Google subscriptions use the store's cancellation timing.
