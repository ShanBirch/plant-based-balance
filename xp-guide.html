How to Earn XP

XP levels your character and counts toward XP challenges. Coins are separate and are used for shop and challenge entry systems.

Challenge leaderboards track the XP you earn after joining. Amounts below are base amounts; if a 2x boost is active, Balance shows the doubled amount when the XP is awarded.

Fast daily route

  1. 1Log meals, hit nutrition targets, and share one logged meal.+18
  2. 2Log a workout, then share the workout card.+2
  3. 3Post one regular Feed check-in and comment on someone else's post.+4
  4. 4Finish a daily quiz at 100% and hit 10,000 synced Fitbit steps.+7

XP Actions

These are the current Balance XP sources and their normal caps.

Meals and Nutrition

  • +1Accepted meal log. Verified photo, AI, or accepted meal-log routes can award this.
  • +1On-time meal. Log within 30 minutes of a scheduled meal reminder.
  • +2Daily nutrition target. Log at least one meal and finish within 20% of calories, protein, carbs, and fat.
  • +15Meal Feed share. Share one logged meal card to Feed per Brisbane day.

Feed and Community

  • +2Daily Feed check-in. One regular Feed post can earn this each day.
  • +2Comment on someone else's post. Comments on your own post do not count.
  • +2AI-verified workout image post. A regular Feed photo can earn this when Balance verifies workout content.
  • +1Nudge an inactive friend. Capped at once per friend per week when they have been inactive long enough.

Training

  • +1Workout logged. Eligible workout log routes award XP.
  • +1Workout card share. Share the workout card to Feed; eligible group-chat shares can also award once per workout session.
  • +1New personal best. New PBs and volume PRs can add XP.
  • ScoreWorkout challenges. Some challenge types score workouts, weight lifted, reps, or calories instead of direct XP.

Progress and Weigh-ins

  • +10Weekly progress photos. Earn once when the weekly progress photo set is accepted.
  • +10Share weekly progress. Share that weekly progress photo set to Feed once.
  • +1Daily weigh-in. Add your weigh-in for the day.
  • +10Friday weigh-in down. Earn when Friday weight is down from the previous Friday in a challenge.
  • +10Friday Feed card. Post the Friday weigh-in review card to Feed once per Friday weigh-in.

Daily Check-ins

  • +1Fitness diary. Submit the daily fitness diary card.
  • +1All three mood check-ins. Complete morning, afternoon, and evening mood check-ins in the same day.
  • +210,000 Fitbit steps. Synced Fitbit steps can award once per day.

Health IQ

  • +1New lesson complete. Health IQ lessons need 100% completion to award XP, with a daily lesson limit.
  • +2Unit complete. Finish all lessons in a unit.
  • +5Module complete. Finish a full module.
  • +5Daily quiz. Score 100% on the daily quiz, once per day.
  • BonusHealth IQ level-ups. Level bonuses are +5, +10, +15, +20, +25, +30, +40, +50, then +100 XP.

Weekly Goals

  • +10Each completed weekly goal. Paid through Weekly Wrapped after goal progress is calculated.
  • +20All three goals hit. Bonus for hitting 3/3 selected weekly goals.
  • Cap50 XP weekly max. Weekly goal rewards are capped at 50 XP total.

Milestones and Streaks

  • +5First meal or first workout. The first accepted meal and first logged workout have milestone bonuses.
  • +1010 meals. Meal tracking milestone.
  • +2550 meals or 50 workouts. Count milestone.
  • +50100 meals or 100 workouts. Count milestone.
  • +100365 meals or 365 workouts. One-year count milestone.
  • BonusPost streaks. 7, 14, 30, 60, and 100-day streak bonuses are +5, +10, +25, +50, and +100 XP.

Boosts and Challenges

  • +200Win a challenge. Challenge wins can award XP and unlock a 30-day 2x XP boost.
  • 2xDouble XP windows. Active boosts can multiply eligible rewards when the app shows the boost.
  • +1Walkthrough checkpoints. Selected onboarding walkthrough checkpoints can award XP until the account reaches the Level 4 starter target.

Caps and fairness

  • Most XP events only pay once per source item, day, workout, week, or challenge window.
  • Photo-based XP can be rejected for low confidence, duplicates, unclear content, or old/future timestamps.
  • Calories challenges require every meal used for the daily nutrition bonus to have a verified photo.
  • Daily Feed check-in XP is capped at one regular Feed post per day, meal Feed share XP is capped at one logged meal per Brisbane day, and comment XP only applies to someone else's post.
  • Progress photo, Friday weigh-in, weekly goal, nudge, and Fitbit rewards follow their listed daily or weekly caps.
