Weekly Goals

Each week has a focus, not a giant to-do list.

You choose up to three goals. Balance tracks the proof, then your weekly review turns it into the next adjustment.

Training Workouts completed, active days, volume, consistency, or a specific movement focus.
Food Protein, calories, meal logging, hydration, fibre, or the simple food habit that matters now.
Recovery Sleep, soreness, stress, cycle awareness, lighter days, and knowing when to pull back.
Confidence Progress photos, learning, sharing wins, and rebuilding trust in yourself through evidence.
About Your Coach

I'm Shannon.

I grew up on Tamborine Mountain in the Gold Coast hinterland, spent years in freestyle BMX, broke both knees, then rebuilt my own relationship with training from the ground up.

I studied Exercise Science, worked as a personal trainer in Melbourne, and owned a weight-training studio in Hampton for eight years. Balance is the coaching system I wish more people had: practical, personal, and built around what actually happens each week.

I personally eat vegetarian, but coaching is built around how you actually eat. Meat, fish, dairy, eggs, plants, all of it can fit if the plan is honest and repeatable.
