Privacy Policy

Updated: April 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Balance: Fitness Gamified mobile application ("App"), owned and operated by FITGotchi ("we", "us", or "our"). It describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use the App:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, and profile information you provide when registering.
  • Health & Fitness Data: Weight, body measurements, workout logs, calorie intake, step counts, heart rate, and sleep data. This may be read from or written to Apple Health with your permission.
  • Photos & Media: Images or videos you choose to upload to your profile or the social feed.
  • Location Data: Approximate location used to display local weather conditions on your dashboard. This is only accessed when the App is in use and with your permission.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, including features used and sessions completed.
  • Mood & Wellbeing Data: Mood, energy, and stress check-ins you voluntarily complete.
  • User-Generated Content: Posts, comments, and messages shared within the App's social features.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise your fitness and nutrition experience.
  • To power gamification features including XP, leaderboards, battles, and FitCoins.
  • To enable social features including the feed, friend challenges, and messaging.
  • To sync fitness data with Apple Health where you have granted permission.
  • To display contextual weather information based on your location.
  • To improve the App and troubleshoot issues.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider with industry-standard encryption. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Apple HealthKit — for reading and writing fitness and health data (with your permission).
  • Supabase — for secure data storage and authentication.
  • Netlify — for hosting the App's web interface.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us. You can also revoke HealthKit or location permissions at any time through your iPhone's Settings app.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 13+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes within the App or by email.
