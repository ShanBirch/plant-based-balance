## Intent

Create a fast, animated vertical ad that shows Balance as a complete fitness app.
The viewer should move from the real home screen through planning, learning,
sharing, activity tracking, coaching, and friendly competition.

## Assets

- Use Shannon's current July 24 Balance screenshots and the real Balance logo.

## Customizations

- Seven product beats: home, weekly goals, learning, community sharing, Activity Insights, coaching, and a challenge with friends.
- Activity Insights cycles rapidly through calories burned, lifting volume, sleep, daily calories, and steps.
- UI cards stack and transform into the Balance logo for the final lockup.
- No voiceover. Use tactile clicks, swipes, whooshes, and a compact electronic music bed if available.

## Notes

- Vertical 9:16 Instagram Reel.
- 15 seconds.
- Keep all critical text clear of the Instagram top header, bottom caption area, and right-side action rail.
- Public-facing copy must not mention AI or automation.
- End on the Balance logo, centred "BALANCE" wordmark, and "Join Balance" call to action.
