The Balance Smoothie Guide

Delicious, grounding recipes to stop the jitters and stabilize blood sugar.

1. Cortisol-Control Berry Blast

Antioxidant Shield
  • 1 cup frozen wild blueberries
  • 1 handful spinach
  • 1/4 avocado
  • 1 scoop vanilla pea protein
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1.5 cups almond milk

2. The "Zen Green" Rescue

Cooling & Hydrating
  • 1/2 cucumber, 1/2 pear
  • 1 inch fresh ginger
  • Squeeze of lime
  • 1 tbsp hemp seeds
  • Coconut water base

3. Cacao "Night Calm"

Serotonin Boost
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp raw cacao powder
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • Pinch sea salt
  • Oat milk base

4. Golden Anti-Inflammatory

Joint Relief
  • 1/2 cup frozen mango
  • 1 tsp turmeric + pinch pepper
  • 1 scoop unflavored protein
  • 1 tsp flax oil
  • Coconut milk base

5. Creamy Avocado Lime

Healthy Fats
  • 1/2 ripe avocado
  • Zest & juice of 1 lime
  • 1 serving vanilla protein
  • Handful of spinach
  • Unsweetened cashew milk

6. Cherry Chamomile Sleep

Natural Melatonin
  • 1 cup frozen tart cherries
  • 1/2 cup chamomile tea (chilled)
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tsp honey (optional)
  • Almond milk base

7. Pumpkin Pie Protein

Zinc & Magnesium
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin pure
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tsp pumpkin spice
  • 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
  • Vanilla protein & oat milk

8. Matcha Mint Energy

L-Theanine Focus
  • 1 tsp matcha powder
  • Fresh mint leaves
  • 1/2 frozen banana
  • Vanilla protein
  • Coconut milk base

9. Beet & Berry Detox

Liver Support
  • 1/4 raw beet (peeled)
  • 1 cup mixed berries
  • 1/2 lemon (juiced)
  • 1 tsp flax seeds
  • Coconut water base

10. Banana Nut Pre-Workout

Sustained Fuel
  • 1 large frozen banana
  • 1/4 cup rolled oats
  • 1 tbsp walnut butter
  • Dash of cinnamon
  • Almond milk base
