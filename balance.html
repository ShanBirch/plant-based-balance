The four-part Balance journey

Learn. Master. Become. Lead.

Start by understanding change. Build the skills to guide yourself, practise them in real life, then help someone else take their next step.

Part 1 · Start hereLearn+

Understand change and build your foundations.

A guided six-week course to understand your habits and turn learning into practical training, food and weekly goals, with check-ins and plan review from Shannon.

What you study each week

6 weeks · Start with the introductory lesson

  1. Week 1

    Why change feels hard

    Past experience, predictions and how new experiences help you learn.

  2. Week 2

    Work with your energy

    Understand why sleep, stress and low energy change your choices so you can plan for the days that usually throw you off.

  3. Week 3

    Build a rhythm that sticks

    Use repetition, smaller actions and your surroundings to make the habits you want easier to keep doing.

  4. Week 4

    Take the fight out of food

    Cravings, emotional eating and recognising the need behind the feeling.

  5. Week 5

    Make progress easier to repeat

    How routines, surroundings and other people shape your habits.

  6. Week 6

    Build your sustainable way forward

    Bring the food basics together into a flexible approach that supports your goal without rigid rules or all-or-nothing thinking.

Part 2Master+

Build the skills to guide your own training and food.

Learn anatomy, exercise technique, programming, nutrition and recovery. Put it into practice by building your own workout program and a week of meals.

What you study each week

10 weeks · A new week opens every seven days

  1. Week 1

    Know the muscles you train

    Connect a muscle to its job and an exercise.

  2. Week 2

    Understand compound lifts

    Film a squat, hinge, push and pull for Shannon to review.

  3. Week 3

    Choose exercises for your muscles

    Explain your exercise choices and suitable alternatives.

  4. Week 4

    Set your training dose

    Choose sets, reps, rest and a progression rule.

  5. Week 5

    Build your workout program

    Save a repeatable week with exercises and progression.

  6. Week 6

    Fit cardio and recovery into your plan

    Make room for conditioning, sleep and recovery.

  7. Week 7

    Learn how to build a meal

    Choose foods and portions that support your day.

  8. Week 8

    Check variety and essential nutrients

    Review your food choices before building the full week.

  9. Week 9

    Design one week of meals

    Save seven days with shopping, prep and a backup.

  10. Week 10

    Review and adjust your plans

    Explain what you would keep and what you would change.

Part 3Become+

Practise the person you are becoming.

Turn training, food and recovery into a repeatable way of living. Shape your environment, reflect on real progress and build a sustainable rhythm for sharing your journey through the Balance Feed and Instagram.

What you study each week

6 weeks · Practical actions, Feed and Instagram

  1. Week 1

    Train your inputs

    Understand how attention trains your feed and shapes what starts to feel normal.

  2. Week 2

    Keep content downstream of the work

    Build posts and Stories from training, food and real life.

  3. Week 3

    Audit your algorithm

    Remove noisy inputs and deliberately reinforce the environment you want.

  4. Week 4

    Build a weekly rhythm

    Choose a posting rhythm that fits around the work instead of replacing it.

  5. Week 5

    Tell one useful story

    Give the evidence meaning by explaining what happened, what helped and what comes next.

  6. Week 6

    Keep the pattern

    Choose the smallest identity and content rhythm you can sustain.

Part 4Lead+

Help someone else take their next step.

Lead through your example, invite a small shared challenge and practise encouragement. Learn to support someone through setbacks without taking over, and contribute to the community.

What you study each week

6 practical weeks · Work through them at your pace

  1. Week 1

    Lead through the example you set

    Choose the values and actions you want to demonstrate.

  2. Week 2

    Invite someone into a small challenge

    Agree on a manageable action and check-in together.

  3. Week 3

    Encourage without taking over

    Listen, recognise effort and keep ownership with the other person.

  4. Week 4

    Support someone through a setback

    Help someone find their next step while respecting your role.

  5. Week 5

    Make your community stronger

    Contribute useful support and respect other people’s privacy.

  6. Week 6

    Keep a sustainable supporting role

    Reflect on your actions and choose a commitment you can maintain.

Balance Learn is part one. The six-week Learn package on this page covers your starting course and support. Master, Become and Lead describe the wider journey beyond those first six weeks.
