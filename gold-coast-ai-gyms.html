A few things you might be wondering.

Do we need to replace our current software?

We'll start by looking at what you already use. Sometimes a better process or a small connection is enough. Available integrations and any extra tool costs are checked before a project is quoted.

Can AI help with gym programming?

Yes. We can create drafting tools for training blocks, session variations and challenges using your coaching rules, equipment and member needs. Coaches keep responsibility for suitability and final approval.

What happens to our member information?

The free review can work from a description of your process. Please don't email member health records, payment details or passwords. Before any implementation, we'll agree the information needed, access permissions and who can see it.

What does implementation cost?

It depends on the workflow and tools involved. You'll receive a scope and quote before any paid work, including any ongoing software costs. There is no obligation to buy after the free review.
