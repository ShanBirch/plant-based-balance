# Visual frame

## Direction

An editorial training ledger built from real gym footage and dated coaching receipts. The reel should feel athletic, precise and earned: warm charcoal, paper-white, Balance gold, one restrained signal-red accent, condensed display type and monospaced evidence labels.

## Canvas and safety

- 1080 x 1920, 9:16.
- Keep every critical word and number inside the conservative Instagram Reel playback-safe band: x 64-870 and y 250-1550.
- Treat the top profile/audio chrome, right action rail and bottom username/caption/navigation area as reserved. Background footage may extend behind them; essential text may not.
- Apply a realistic Instagram Reel UI overlay to every text-bearing scene after its longest line and largest animation state have entered. A clean export or cover crop alone is not approval evidence.
- Cover-safe title stays in the central grid crop.
- Real message wording is presented as editorial quote cards, never as a fake Instagram UI.

## Palette

- Ink: `#0F1110`
- Paper: `#F4F0E6`
- Balance gold: `#D7A94C`
- Signal red: `#D4573B`
- Muted steel: `#9AA09B`

## Type

- Display: `Impact`, `Arial Narrow`, sans-serif.
- Evidence and labels: `Consolas`, `Courier New`, monospace.
- Body: `Arial`, sans-serif.

## Motion language

- Weight figures land with short scale-and-settle hits.
- Dates and quotes arrive as clipped ledger strips.
- Progression scenes cut on the music and connect with a thin gold route line.
- The Balance mark closes with the default glimmer treatment.
- No decorative camera movement over footage; the lifts remain the hero.
