# Week-five Feed experiment

Shannon approved replacing the conversation action with three Feed posts about meals, workouts or walks, comments on three other posts, and a weekly check-in about the effect on motivation or behaviour. No positive outcome or PB is required. A PB share can be one of the three posts.

The existing five-task structure remains: meals, workouts, practical experiment, Feed participation, weekly check-in. The old separate PB requirement becomes Feed participation. Other weeks are unchanged. The social lesson's action paragraph and two matching quiz questions now teach this action; its research explanation is unchanged.

## When ticks appear

- Feed participation: saved posts by the member within their course-week interval, plus nonempty comments on three distinct posts owned by other members. Multiple comments on one post count once. Replies on the member's own post and out-of-week activity do not count. The progress hint displays both counts. Topic relevance and meaningful engagement are assessed in the action review, not inferred from a raw count.
- Practical experiment: the weekly check-in includes what was shared, what the interactions were like, and whether motivation/actions changed. The server fetches and saves the owned, dated Feed evidence with the report. AI review requires the activity evidence and grounded check-in evidence, including relevance and meaningful participation. A coach can review the record; approval is blocked if the saved activity evidence is incomplete. No change in motivation is valid.
- Weekly check-in: continues to tick on submission separately from the experiment review. Saving only a reflection does not complete the experiment.
- Earlier completed experiments and PB Feed tasks retain credit. Old completed experiment instructions remain visible in the action view. No member records were rewritten for rollout.

Validation: focused course/review tests, live read-only Feed/comment join, and browser action/check-in inspection across small portrait and landscape, light/dark, zero and nonzero top/bottom safe-area simulations, including scroll/close/reopen. No member check-ins or Feed content were created during QA.
