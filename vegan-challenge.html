About Your Coach

I'm Shannon.

I've been vegetarian since I was born, raised on Tamborine Mountain in the Gold Coast hinterland. I've also dabbled in vegan diets for two years, so I know how to do plant-based well. As a kid I was deep into freestyle BMX. Did it for years until I broke both knees and had to find a different way to stay strong.

So I went to uni, got my Bachelor of Exercise Science, and moved to Melbourne. Spent eight years there as a personal trainer, eventually owning my own weight-training studio in Hampton, where I ran three classes a day and lived upstairs with my rabbit, Sunshine.

Now I'm back on the Gold Coast, where I built Balance, the app behind this challenge, to prove what I've known my whole life: plant-based fitness works. The 30-day challenge is your way in. No cost, no catch.
