The 10-Minute Workout

No time? No problem. Use this EMOM circuit.

Consistency triggers results, not duration. You can get an effective metabolic boost in just 10 minutes with an EMOM (Every Minute on the Minute) style workout.

The Circuit

Set a timer for 10 minutes. At the start of every minute, do the following. Rest for the remainder of the minute.

  • Min 1: 15 Squats
  • Min 2: 10 Pushups (knees or toes)
  • Min 3: 20 Jumping Jacks (or low impact step-outs)
  • Min 4: 30 second Plank
  • Min 5: Rest & Repeat

Do this twice for a 10-minute burn that keeps your metabolism elevated for hours.
