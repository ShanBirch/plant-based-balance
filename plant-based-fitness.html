What you'll learn

One idea at a time.
Build your understanding.

Begin with brain basics, then explore prediction, experience, energy, habits and food. There are six lessons in Week 1 and four or five in later weeks, with no required practical tasks.

Balance Learn: The Ten-Week Course

WEEK 1Meet your brain⌄

Meet the researchers, explore how your brain constructs experience, then learn about brain cells, messages, the body and senses. 6 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 2How your brain predicts⌄

Understand expectations, past experience and the predictions behind familiar choices. 4 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 3Experience shapes reality⌄

Follow one gym experience through sensory signals, learned concepts, emotion and context. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 4Work with your energy⌄

Understand how sleep, stress and bodily needs influence your choices. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 5The prediction-action loop⌄

Explore the free-energy principle: infer what is happening, weigh the evidence and act to learn or meet a need. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 6Build a rhythm that sticks⌄

Use small actions and repetition to practise a helpful routine. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 7What actually is learning?⌄

Follow the model update: expectation, surprising evidence, confidence weighting and a changed prediction next time. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 8Take the fight out of food⌄

Understand cravings and the context around eating without judging yourself. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 9Make progress easier to repeat⌄

Shape routines, surroundings and support around the choices you want to repeat. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

WEEK 10Build your sustainable way forward⌄

Bring energy, protein, carbohydrates and fats together in a flexible meal. 5 short lessons, each followed by a quiz.

About the course.

Is Learn the same as Summer Ready Shred?

No. Learn is the ten-week course included within the ten-week Summer Ready Shred. The challenge brings the learning together with workouts, food support and accountability.

How do I access Learn?

Learn is included with Balance coaching and Summer Ready Shred. It is no longer sold as a separate upfront or weekly purchase. Existing members keep their agreed access.

What is the aim?

To understand your habits and build practical skills for long-term lifestyle change. You apply the lessons to real situations, rather than relying on a perfect week or a rigid plan.
