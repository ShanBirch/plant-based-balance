Before you join

Questions worth answering.

Do I have to be vegan?

No. You can be plant-based, moving that way or simply comfortable using plant-based nutrition tools and training in a plant-based community.

Is my plan fully personalised?

The Founders Pass includes personal coaching support, questions, direction and help choosing your starting rhythm. It is not unlimited daily coaching or a weekly fully customised plan-review service. Those options are available separately.

What does lifetime access mean?

You keep access to the core Balance app and community for as long as Balance continues operating those services. Your coaching introduction with Shannon lasts six weeks.

Can I train from home?

Yes. Your suggested starting structure can account for your available equipment, schedule and current capacity.
