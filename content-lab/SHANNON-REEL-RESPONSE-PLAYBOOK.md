# Shannon Reel Response Playbook

Use this alongside the content edit memory when scripting, reviewing, or revising Shannon-led Reels. It turns TRIBE response timelines and real platform results into reusable Shannon-specific language and delivery knowledge.

## Non-negotiable hierarchy

1. Meaning and factual completeness.
2. Shannon's natural voice and a coherent persuasive arc.
3. Offer, evidence, and CTA clarity.
4. Response-model optimization.
5. Platform validation.

TRIBE identifies moments worth investigating. It never authorizes a cut that breaks a sentence, removes necessary reasoning, weakens an offer, or makes the Reel confusing. A quieter setup may be necessary for a later payoff.

## Phrase-level learning loop

For each analyzed Reel:

1. Align every response second with the exact transcript, word timing, delivery, visual state, caption, music, and edit.
2. Tag each phrase by rhetorical job: recognition, shame relief, reframe, tension, curiosity, analogy, mechanism, proof, agency, offer, price, risk reversal, or CTA.
3. Record whether the response rose, fell, or stayed flat before, during, and after the phrase. Do not credit the words when a cut, facial expression, visual reveal, caption, or musical rise changed simultaneously.
4. Form one explicit editing or scripting hypothesis.
5. Compare meaning-equivalent variants while holding delivery and visuals as constant as practical.
6. Validate the winner against Instagram/Facebook retention, completion, replays, saves, shares, DMs, clicks, leads, and sales.
7. Promote a phrase pattern to a Shannon default only after it repeats across more than one context or is supported by platform behavior. Record weak or contradictory evidence too.

## Current provisional patterns

These are hypotheses, not universal rules:

| Phrase or structure | Job | Current evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Specific failed behavior -> remove blame -> reveal the structural cause -> restore agency | Recognition and relief | The black-shirt ad responded strongly to `That doesn't necessarily mean you're lazy` and `the plan only works when life is perfect` | Provisional |
| `The plan only works when life is perfect` | Concrete reframe | Strong early response in the black-shirt ad | Provisional |
| Clear price + `no subscription, no auto-renewal` | Risk reversal | Response recovered strongly at the price section | Provisional |
| `Message me BALANCE right now` plus a concrete next step | Direct CTA | Strongest response period in the black-shirt ad | Provisional |
| Long spoken inclusion lists | Offer detail | Weakest sustained period in the black-shirt ad; may be better shown visually while the speech states the organizing benefit | Provisional risk |

Do not mechanically repeat these exact words in every Reel. Reuse the underlying emotional and logical function, then write a fresh Shannon-native line for the topic.

## Experiment record

For every test, save:

- Reel and version IDs.
- Exact transcript with word timestamps.
- Phrase tags and delivery notes.
- Visual, caption, edit, and music state at each tested phrase.
- TRIBE timeline and the proxy definition used to summarize it.
- Original-versus-revision hypothesis.
- Platform metrics by version and audience.
- Decision: keep, reject, retest, or provisional pattern.

Never call the derived TRIBE proxy an official attention, persuasion, retention, or conversion score. It is a model-response diagnostic. Real audience behavior decides whether a phrase works commercially.

## Revision gate

Before showing a response-optimized edit, watch it completely with sound and confirm:

- Every sentence and join is grammatical and semantically complete.
- The viewer can explain the Reel's main point afterward.
- Required mechanism, evidence, offer, inclusions, price, risk reversal, and CTA remain clear where relevant.
- Overlays and captions still prove or clarify what the voice says.
- The revision improves the intended response arc rather than merely adding stimulation.
