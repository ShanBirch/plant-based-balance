Built by Shannon

Why does change stick for some people, but not others?

I'm an exercise scientist and former gym owner. I've worked with hundreds of clients. Some changed their lives. Others struggled to make it stick. That left me wondering why. Was it really about being lazy or trying harder, or was something deeper going on?

Studying neuroscience led me to look more closely at the habits and automatic responses that shape our everyday choices. I built Balance to help people understand that “autopilot” and practise making changes in their own lives.

Balance Learn is the introductory six-week course, part one of four. It brings those ideas into your training, food and daily routines, with practical actions and support from me.

It's more than a meal plan or workout program. It's knowledge you can put to work, with guidance as you practise. The aim is to build skills you can keep using long after these six weeks.

The four-part Balance journey

Learn. Master. Become. Lead.

Start by understanding change. Build the skills to guide yourself, practise them in real life, then help someone else take their next step.

  1. Part 1 · Start here

    Learn

    Understand change and build your foundations.

    A guided six-week course to understand your habits and turn learning into practical training, food and weekly goals, with check-ins and plan review from Shannon.

  2. Part 2

    Master

    Build the skills to guide your own training and food.

    Learn anatomy, exercise technique, programming, nutrition and recovery. Put it into practice by building your own workout program and a week of meals.

  3. Part 3

    Become

    Practise the person you are becoming.

    Turn training, food and recovery into a repeatable way of living. Shape your environment, reflect on real progress and build a sustainable rhythm for sharing your journey through the Balance Feed and Instagram.

  4. Part 4

    Lead

    Help someone else take their next step.

    Lead through your example, invite a small shared challenge and practise encouragement. Learn to support someone through setbacks without taking over, and contribute to the community.

Balance Learn is part one. The six-week Learn package on this page covers your starting course and support. Master, Become and Lead describe the wider journey beyond those first six weeks.

Is this you?

A good fit if you want a real system, not a fitness identity test.

Balance Learn is for you if:

  • You know some of what to do but struggle to keep doing it when life changes.
  • You feel overwhelmed by all-or-nothing plans or keep restarting after a missed day.
  • You want a clear weekly course, workout structure and practical accountability.
  • You are willing to complete the lessons, weekly actions and Saturday check-in.

It is probably not for you if:

  • You want instant replies or unlimited daily one-to-one coaching.
  • You need medical, injury or clinical nutrition treatment.
  • You only want a PDF and never plan to use the app or community.
  • You expect results without completing the training and check-ins.

Questions people usually ask.

What happens after the six weeks?

The AUD $149 upfront option finishes after six weeks and does not renew automatically. The AUD $24.83 weekly option has a six-week minimum, then continues weekly with the same coaching support until you cancel before your next payment.

What coaching is included?

For six weeks, you receive one weekly check-in plus a workout and food review with practical adjustments from Shannon. You can also ask questions inside Balance. It does not include instant replies, unlimited daily access or live weekly calls.

Can the meal support fit plant-based eating?

Yes. Meal-plan support can fit plant-based eating and other recorded dietary needs. Plant-based eating is an available support option, not a requirement or the focus of the course.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Choose full gym training, at-home dumbbell workouts, bodyweight sessions with no equipment, or resistance band training. Balance also includes yoga, recovery and mobility sessions. Your starting structure can be matched to the equipment you have available. See the training options.

Is the character the main point of the app?

No. Gamification is there to make consistency more enjoyable. Your workouts, nutrition, progress and community remain the main experience.
