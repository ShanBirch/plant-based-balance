Before you start.

Do I need a Zoom session?

Balance Learn is for people who can exercise independently: its weekly 45-minute Zoom session is a teaching lesson. Zoom Personal Training replaces that lesson with a 45-minute supervised workout. Programming & Nutrition has no live session.

What happens after the starting package?

Talk with Shannon about your next steps. Separate ongoing coaching has a three-month minimum at $75/week for Programming & Nutrition or $125/week with one weekly 45-minute Zoom PT session. A repeat upfront fee or automatic renewal is not assumed. Existing members keep their agreed access and terms.

How do I choose?

Talk with Shannon about your goals, routine and the support you need. We'll confirm the package, commitment and payment arrangements before you commit.
