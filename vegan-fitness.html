Is this you?

A good fit if you want a real system, not a fitness identity test.

The Founders Pass is for you if:

  • You are vegan, plant-based or moving that way.
  • You want to get stronger, leaner or simply more consistent.
  • You want training structure without paying for weekly personal coaching.
  • You would use a community that understands how you eat and why.

It is probably not for you if:

  • You want daily one-to-one access to Shannon.
  • You need medical, injury or clinical nutrition treatment.
  • You only want a PDF and never plan to use the app or community.
  • You expect results without completing the training and check-ins.
Clear before you join

Questions people usually ask.

What does lifetime access mean?

It means access to the core Balance app and community for as long as Balance continues operating those services. It does not include every future paid specialist service, third-party charge or ongoing personal coaching.

Is personal coaching included forever?

No. The Founders Pass includes the guided six-week kickstart, app tools and community accountability. Individual weekly plan reviews and adjustments from Shannon are available separately through Starter Coaching.

Do I need to be an experienced vegan?

No. The community is designed for vegans and plant-based people at different stages. You do need to be comfortable training in a vegan-first environment.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Balance supports home and gym training, and the starting structure can be matched to the equipment you have available.

Is the character the main point of the app?

No. Gamification is there to make consistency more enjoyable. Your workouts, nutrition, progress and community remain the main experience.
