# Week 5 Electric Challenge System

Deep navy is the continuous world. Royal blue supplies structure and speed; electric aqua marks live proof; warm gold appears only on the week count and completion milestone. The frame should feel like a sports broadcast title sequence, not an app demo or sales presentation.

Use oversized edge-anchored type, asymmetric media zones, visible blue planes, electric rules and perspective. Motion is percussive and directional: slams, shutters, depth pivots, count-ups and snap-to-grid landings. Avoid warm-white canvases, centered cream cards, soft coaching-preview assembly, sales CTAs, gradients in text, and repeated floating dashboard grids.
