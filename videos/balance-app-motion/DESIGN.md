# Balance App Motion Design

## Style Prompt

Premium, tactile app-product motion with the confidence of a training dashboard and the warmth of personal coaching. Real Balance screens live inside dimensional phone cards on a warm cream canvas with gold accents. A large transparent Balance harmony logo sits quietly behind the moving cards. The seven product beats move quickly before resolving into the calm final logo lockup.

## Colors

- Background: `#F7F2E8`
- Surface: `#FFF9ED`
- Warm gold accent: `#D8A43A`
- Progress gold: `#A96F00`
- Final canvas: `#F7F2E8`
- Light text: `#FFFFFF`
- Dark text: `#160D20`

## Typography

- Feature verbs and final lockup: Montserrat, weight 900.
- Small progress labels and metadata: Space Mono, weight 700.
- Minimum Instagram Reel sizes: 60px headlines, 35px supporting copy, 24px labels.

## Motion Rules

- One dominant motif: real app cards swipe into place, with the community fan and rapid Activity Insights sequence as controlled variations.
- Use explicit seek-safe `fromTo()` entrances.
- Use tactile scale punches, short parallax shifts, and one circular ivory reveal.
- Give the Balance logo a tasteful gold glimmer sweep whenever it appears, including two restrained passes on the final lockup.
- Keep the final brand lockup still enough to read through the 15-second finish.

## What NOT to Do

- No generic neon-tech gradients.
- No invented app features or fake statistics.
- No voiceover, AI language, or automation language.
- No tiny web-sized labels or equal-weight card grid.
- No jump cuts, uncontrolled bounce, or multiple competing transition styles.
- No full-screen linear gradients on the dark canvas.
