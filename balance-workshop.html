A few useful details.

Is this an exercise class?

No. It is a seated educational workshop with practical planning. Exercise is one everyday example, and no supervised workout is included.

Does it cover mental health?

We discuss stress, energy and everyday wellbeing within general exercise and behaviour education. This workshop does not provide mental health treatment or individual clinical advice.

What happens after the workshop?

Participants keep their worksheet and can review their own plan over the following week. Ongoing coaching, app access and follow-up sessions are not included in this workshop estimate.
