What you actually do each week

A simple rhythm with a clear Saturday review.

Every week follows the same understandable loop. You are never expected to absorb the whole course at once or guess what to do next.

1. Learn the week's idea

Complete the five short guided lessons in Course. Each lesson connects the behaviour-change idea to a decision you face in normal life.

2. Choose the week's actions

Turn the lessons into 1 to 3 Weekly Goals and a small set of practical course actions, so the week stays focused.

3. Practise inside real life

Follow your workout plan, use meal support, and record the useful evidence the course asks for. Feed gives you a place to share a reflection, action or win.

Every Saturday

Complete your weekly check-in. Shannon reviews your training and food, then makes practical adjustments for the next week.

At completion

When the required prelude, all 30 weekly Balance Learn lessons and the required weekly course actions are genuinely complete, Balance issues your named Certificate of Completion with your course completion date. It is not an accredited qualification.

Built by Shannon

I wanted people to understand why they keep restarting, then have a practical way forward.

I am an exercise scientist and former gym owner. I built Balance Learn to translate behaviour-change and neuroscience ideas into decisions you can use during an ordinary, imperfect week.

Balance Learn is a supported six-week curriculum with me. You receive one Saturday check-in, workout and food review, and practical adjustment each week. It is not a subscription, and the program finishes after week six without renewing automatically.

Is this you?

A good fit if you want a real system, not a fitness identity test.

Balance Learn is for you if:

  • You know some of what to do but struggle to keep doing it when life changes.
  • You feel overwhelmed by all-or-nothing plans or keep restarting after a missed day.
  • You want a clear weekly course, workout structure and practical accountability.
  • You are willing to complete the lessons, weekly actions and Saturday check-in.

It is probably not for you if:

  • You want instant replies or unlimited daily one-to-one coaching.
  • You need medical, injury or clinical nutrition treatment.
  • You only want a PDF and never plan to use the app or community.
  • You expect results without completing the training and check-ins.

Questions people usually ask.

What happens after the six weeks?

Balance Learn is not a subscription. It finishes after week six and does not renew automatically. You can decide what you want to do next without another payment being taken.

What coaching is included?

For six weeks, you receive one weekly check-in plus a workout and food review with practical adjustments from Shannon. You can also ask questions inside Balance. It does not include instant replies, unlimited daily access or live weekly calls.

Can the meal support fit plant-based eating?

Yes. Meal-plan support can fit plant-based eating and other recorded dietary needs. Plant-based eating is an available support option, not a requirement or the focus of the course.

Can I train at home?

Yes. Balance supports home and gym training, and the starting structure can be matched to the equipment you have available.

Is the character the main point of the app?

No. Gamification is there to make consistency more enjoyable. Your workouts, nutrition, progress and community remain the main experience.
