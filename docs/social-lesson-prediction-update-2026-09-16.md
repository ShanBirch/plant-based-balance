# Requested social-lesson explanation update

After the original lesson restoration, Shannon explicitly requested a fuller explanation of prediction, body/environment feedback and the free-energy principle in People Are Your Strongest Environment (mind-6-5), its quiz, and the draft app-walkthrough reel.

Only that approved exception is changed. The other 21 restored lessons remain byte-for-byte original and are protected by the restoration tests. Two questions were added, bringing this lesson to eight: the technical meaning of surprise, and how learned expectations, sensory evidence and action relate. Existing social-study, non-guarantee and Feed-action questions remain.

The explanation identifies the free-energy principle as a theoretical framework, distinguishes information-theoretic surprise from feeling startled, includes body and environmental signals and the role of action, and separates the proposed interpretation from evidence of social influence. The 57% statistic remains a relative association, not a proven brain mechanism. Three Feed posts and three comments are a practical experiment, not a validated neurological dose. No automatic fitness or social-conformity guarantee is made.

Sources: Friston (2010), https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn2787; Christakis and Fowler (2007), https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMsa066082.
