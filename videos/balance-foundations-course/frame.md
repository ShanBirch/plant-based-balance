# Balance Cream and Gold Editorial

## Concept angle

The curriculum unfolds like a premium field guide rather than a school syllabus.
Every week has one large practical idea, one simple visual model and five exact
lesson titles. A fine gold progress rail connects the chapters so the viewer can
feel that the six weeks build on one another.

## Typography

- Montserrat 900 carries hooks and week titles at 68-112px, with the smaller end reserved for long titles.
- Montserrat 700 carries lesson titles at 28-36px.
- Montserrat 400 carries one short explanatory line at 29-38px.
- Space Mono 700 carries week numbers and counters at 18-26px, with 14-17px reserved for compact model labels.
- Use tight display tracking around -0.045em and generous body line-height around 1.35.

## Frame anatomy

- Opening orientation page: oversized course name first, with no competing promise or curriculum detail.
- Background: warm cream with a very subtle paper grain and one low-opacity gold atmosphere shape.
- Header: `BALANCE / FOUNDATIONS` at the upper safe edge, plus the current week counter.
- Main focal: the week title and its explanatory visual model.
- Secondary focal: five lesson rows, numbered 01-05, in paper cards with a gold-tint border and no shadow.
- Footer: `6 WEEKS / 30 LESSONS` on the left and a six-segment gold progress rail above the Instagram bottom reserve.

## Components

- Lesson row: white or 4% gold tint, 2px gold-tint border, 18px radius, no shadow.
- Week badge: solid gold pill with cream type.
- Model line: 3px solid gold, rounded ends, never a decorative gradient.
- End-card slogan: large near-black setup line followed by one large solid-gold resolution line.
- Logo: official mark only. Give it one tasteful diagonal gold glimmer on the opening and close.

## Motion grammar

- Orient first, explain second: the viewer should know what the video is before the benefit-led hook appears.
- Use oversized, plain-language title copy for the first reading beat; assume the viewer is distracted and watching muted.
- One decisive camera or layout move per scene, then a readable hold.
- Lesson rows arrive in a paced cascade, not all at once.
- Social-proof cards may land in a physical stack at alternating angles, with each previous card remaining visibly present underneath the newest one. When the stack accelerates, shorten both the gaps and landing durations progressively rather than switching abruptly to one repeated fast cadence.
- Visual models draw, fill, connect or re-route; they never float without meaning.
- The cream ground and footer progress rail persist as continuity across hard chapter cuts.
- No bounce chains, random particles, generic gradients, arbitrary blobs or screensaver motion.

## Instagram safety

- Essential content stays inside x=76-920, y=190-1585.
- The rightmost 130px is atmosphere only.
- No critical copy below y=1585.
- The opening hook must remain readable in the centered 1:1 profile-grid crop.

## Content truth

- Week titles, descriptions and lesson titles come from `lib/learning-inline.js` on origin/main.
- The evergreen close uses `UNDERSTAND CHANGE. BUILD WHAT LASTS.` with no price, offer inclusions or sales prompt.
- Never imply instant replies, unlimited support, live calls or guaranteed results.
