# Shannon DM Response Playbook

Use this when auditing, drafting, testing, or learning from Balance lead and client conversations. The purpose is to build a Shannon-specific conversation style from real outcomes without turning DMs into scripts or pressure tactics.

## Outcome hierarchy

Judge language against the strongest verified outcome available:

1. Stripe-confirmed purchase tied to the thread.
2. Checkout handoff after explicit transactional intent.
3. Buyer intent in the person's own words.
4. Offer-ready acknowledgement of wanting help, structure, accountability, community, or coaching.
5. Problem qualification containing both a relevant goal and a real blocker.
6. A later relevant inbound reply.
7. Message delivery or conversation volume.

A reply proves engagement only. It does not prove qualification, trust, offer fit, or commercial effectiveness.

## Conversation learning unit

Never score an isolated outbound line without its episode. Record:

- Acquisition lane: paid Meta, organic inbound, organic outreach, existing client, or support.
- Funnel stage and the exact evidence supporting it.
- The person's complete newest inbound turn and relevant prior context.
- Shannon's or the system's exact outbound response.
- Conversational function: recognition, exact-detail reflection, shame relief, reframe, elicitation, practical help, proof, offer bridge, inclusions, price, objection resolution, autonomy protection, handoff, or pause.
- Shape: statement-led or question-led, number of questions, length, specificity, tone, and number of bubbles.
- The next inbound response, later progression, and strongest eventual outcome.
- Confounds: funnel position, lead warmth, acquisition source, proof media, response timing, Shannon manual involvement, prior unanswered messages, or safety/support context.

## Learning loop

1. Sample complete eligible episodes, including silence, declines, objections, and non-buyers. Do not train only on successful or high-volume threads.
2. Compare language only within similar acquisition lanes, stages, and lead contexts.
3. Tag the job each phrase or sentence performs.
4. Form one specific hypothesis, such as `an exact-detail reflection followed by a statement produces fuller blocker disclosure than a broad question at the same stage`.
5. Test meaning-equivalent variants while holding timing, stage, proof, and offer facts as constant as practical.
6. Measure both the immediate reply and the strongest later canonical outcome.
7. Promote a default only after Shannon explicitly states it, multiple independent clean episodes support it, or safety/truth/consent requires it.

Do not mechanically reuse one apparent winning phrase. Learn the rhetorical function, then write a fresh response from the person's words and live context.

## Ethical and analytical safeguards

- Optimise for accurate understanding, useful progression, fit, and informed choice, not maximum replies or pressure.
- Never manufacture urgency, exploit vulnerability, diagnose, force admissions, hide price, bypass objections, or treat a no as a wording problem.
- Paid-Meta language must not be learned into organic relationship DMs without lane-specific evidence.
- Client and support conversations are not sales-training examples.
- Do not promote patterns from warmth, friendliness, flirtiness, reply count, stage labels, or one unusual person.
- Keep private identifiers and raw conversations out of committed artifacts. Save anonymised patterns, aggregates, and only the minimum exact wording needed for a regression case.
- Never expose internal scoring, AI, automation, or experimentation language to the person in the DM.

## Durable experiment record

For each promoted, rejected, or retested pattern, save:

- Date and anonymised cohort definition.
- Acquisition lane and starting stage.
- Hypothesis and response variants.
- Episode count, exclusions, and confounds.
- Immediate relevant-reply rate.
- Goal/blocker, offer-ready, buyer-intent, checkout, and purchase outcomes where observable.
- Decision: durable, supported, provisional, rejected, or retest.
- Any Shannon wording correction and the skill or prompt updated from it.
