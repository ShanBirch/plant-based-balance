# Balance course structure

Published structure: Learn stays six weeks; Become uses the existing six-week practical Feed/Instagram journey; Master has ten weekly releases; Lead has six self-paced practical weeks about supporting someone else. Existing access after Learn is retained. The four courses are an organising path, not a new purchase or entitlement change.

## Keep each course focused

Only selected lessons move. Learn keeps its original 30 quizzes. Become receives five identity lessons. Master receives 36 anatomy, technique, programming and nutrition lessons as optional deeper learning beside its ten applied weeks. Lead receives Connection and Purpose beside six new support lessons. The remaining 131 lessons stay in seven specialist courses. All 203 existing IDs remain unchanged, so saved lesson completions and historical XP remain intact. A moved lesson is excluded from specialist topic counts and lists, but is still resolvable by its original ID.

## Become

The existing journey weeks 7–12 are now labelled Balance Become: inputs and account planning, documenting real work, curating the feed, a practical posting rhythm, useful stories and maintaining the pattern. Existing journey data, action verification, Instagram planning and Home navigation continue to use their original records. The old balance-identity course link resolves to Become. No progress is copied into the new Lead course.

## Master: ten weekly releases

1. Know the muscles you train.
2. Understand compound lifts and submit squat, hinge, push and pull clips.
3. Choose exercises and useful alternatives.
4. Set sets, reps, rest, effort and progression.
5. Build the workout program in the existing builder.
6. Fit cardio and recovery into the week.
7. Build a balanced meal.
8. Check variety and essential nutrients.
9. Design seven days of meals, shopping and preparation.
10. Review both plans and decide what to adjust.

The member explicitly starts Master. The database records that start time; the client cannot insert a backdated timestamp, update it or delete the enrollment. Week 1 opens immediately and subsequent weeks every seven days. Old completed stages remain reviewable. Existing six-stage answers and completion flags are migrated in memory to weeks 1, 2, 5, 7, 9 and 10; existing workout and meal data are retained. The converted version is saved with the next successful draft save. Browsing the library never starts the clock.

## Lead: six practical weeks

1. Lead through the example you set.
2. Invite someone into a small, agreed challenge.
3. Encourage without taking over.
4. Support someone through a setback.
5. Make the community stronger.
6. Review your supporting identity and a sustainable commitment.

Each week has teaching, a knowledge check and a private practical reflection. Reflections are self-reported evidence; the app does not claim to verify another person's activity or send invitations. Correct answers and useful reflections are required for completion. No certificate, professional qualification or extra XP is created.

## Verification and measurement

Variant: focused_four_part_path_v1. Primary KPI: started Master members completing the workout and meal projects within 12 weeks. Diagnostics: master_course_started, week-level master events (curriculum balance_master_10_weeks_v2), lead_draft_saved and lead_week_completed. Guardrails: save failures, loss of historical completions, duplicate lesson placement, weekly-release errors and form-check review age. First diagnostic review: 2026-09-21; completion review: 2026-11-30. Become retains the existing journey measurement.

Tests cover lesson ownership and the remaining seven specialist courses, week boundaries, legacy Master migration, video receipts, Lead completion, existing Learn actions and journey navigation. Database checks cover private rows, immutable start times and anonymous denial. Phone fixtures verify first-week release, disabled future weeks, saved Lead reflections and readable light/dark layouts. Fixture activity does not create real member posts or messages.
