This page explains how to request deletion of personal information connected with Balance, the Balance website, the Balance - Fitness Gamified app, and related online coaching services operated by Shannon Rhys Birch under the Balance brand.

Request received.

Request Deletion

To request deletion of your Balance account data or related personal information, email shannon@plantbased-balance.org from the email address connected with your Balance account.

If your request relates to Facebook or Instagram data, include the Facebook Page, Instagram account, or email address you used when granting access, so we can identify the relevant record.

What We Delete

  • Account and contact information no longer needed to provide the service.
  • Connected Meta authorization records, where applicable.
  • Stored coaching, support, or app data that is not required for legal, safety, billing, tax, dispute, or fraud-prevention reasons.

Processing Time

We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days. Some records may be retained where required by law, payment records, safety obligations, or legitimate business record keeping.
