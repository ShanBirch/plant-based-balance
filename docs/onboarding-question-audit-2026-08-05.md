# Balance Onboarding Question Audit

The first-run flow should only ask for information that changes safety, calculations, the six-week training start, meal-plan constraints, or Shannon's coaching context.

## Kept in first-run onboarding

| Question | Why Balance needs it |
| --- | --- |
| Her or him setup | Used by the existing calorie calculation and gender-specific app surfaces. |
| First name | Personalises the app and Shannon's coaching handoff. |
| Age, height, and current weight | Required for calorie, macro, hydration, and recovery calculations. |
| What should feel different after six weeks | Captures the person's desired outcome in their own words so Balance can infer a starting direction. |
| What normally knocks them off track | Gives Shannon and the app useful behaviour-change context. |
| Realistic weekly training capacity | Sets the initial two- or three-day training frequency. |
| Least-disrupted routine window | Places the suggested training rhythm into the person's real week. |
| Available equipment | Selects viable workouts. |
| Current activity and energy | Adjusts calorie calculations and the minimum starting session. |
| Injuries, pain, or movements to avoid | Retains essential planning and safety context without diagnosing. |
| Food style and restrictions | Protects meal-plan relevance and allergy/restriction handling. |
| Cycle-aware training | Kept as an optional, female-only choice and defaults to off. |

## Assigned by Balance

- The primary six-week direction is inferred from the person's own outcome sentence.
- The initial workout frequency is two or three sessions based on reliable weekly capacity.
- The starter session minimum is 10, 15, or 20 minutes based on capacity and energy.
- The training split defaults to full body for two or three days and upper/lower for four or more.
- The first three Weekly Goals are created automatically: completed workouts, protein consistency, and one goal matched to nutrition, energy, or general consistency.
- Learning topics are inferred from the outcome rather than requested during setup.

## Removed from first-run onboarding

| Removed question | New handling |
| --- | --- |
| Pick up to three broad goals | Inferred from the six-week outcome answer. |
| Choose three Weekly Goals | Balance creates them automatically and members can edit later. |
| Goal weight | Current weight is used as the neutral calculation baseline; body direction is inferred from the written outcome. |
| Choose a starter session length | Balance assigns a conservative minimum from capacity and energy. |
| Describe every competing priority | Combined into the single real-life blocker question. |
| Choose learning interests | Inferred and editable later through normal course use. |
| Instagram handle | Removed from core setup; acquisition attribution and authenticated account identity already provide the conversion link. |
| Choose a training split | Assigned from frequency and editable later. |
| Exercise likes and dislikes | Removed from first run; actual limitations remain in the safety question and preferences can be edited later. |
| Cuisine, favourite foods, cooking time | Already deferred; these remain optional meal-plan settings rather than onboarding blockers. |
| Character and profile-photo setup | Kept out of the core flow and available later. |
