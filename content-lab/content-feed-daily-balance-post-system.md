# Balance Instagram Content System

This is the canonical operating system for `@shan_n_sunny`.

Last strategy review: 2026-07-28.

Public goal: help plant-based people become fitter, stronger and more consistent.

Commercial goal: make the feed repeatedly and truthfully imply:

> I am not broken. I do not need more willpower. I need a clear plan, useful course correction and Shannon in my corner.

The feed should not feel like a catalogue of unrelated fitness facts. Every post should do one of three jobs:

1. Change what the viewer believes about their problem.
2. Demonstrate how Shannon coaches.
3. Prove that the structure works and make the next step feel natural.

Do not mention AI, automation, models, agents, internal tools or private client systems in public copy.

## The Three-Post Daily Stack

Shannon wants three Instagram posts or deliberate content touches per day. Run this first as a 14-day measured experiment.

The target is not three expensive, fully produced Reels every day. The target is one hero asset plus two lighter supporting assets that form a complete persuasion sequence.

| Window | Job | Default formats | Viewer thought |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Morning | BELIEF | Science Reel, brain Reel, myth or identity post | "That explains why I keep struggling." |
| Midday | COACH | Exercise Reel, form carousel, food or habit framework | "Shannon would know what to change for me." |
| Evening | PROOF | Client-safe proof, Shannon proof, community win, coaching observation or invitation | "People like me are doing this. Maybe I should message him." |

If only one feed post is ready, publish the strongest feed post and use Stories for the other two jobs. Never reduce quality, privacy or factual standards just to hit three feed uploads.

### Conversion rhythm

- BELIEF earns attention and removes shame.
- COACH turns authority into practical help.
- PROOF makes the method tangible.
- The CTA is the smallest useful next step, not a hard pitch on every post.

Use this weekly CTA balance:

- 7 save, share, reply or follow CTAs.
- 7 soft conversation CTAs.
- 4 direct `DM BALANCE` coaching invitations.
- 3 posts with no CTA beyond the useful conclusion.

Do not put `DM BALANCE` on all 21 weekly posts. Repetition should build a belief, not create banner blindness.

## The Six-Slot Creative Loop

The old rotation was useful for variety but left no dedicated conversion bridge. Use this loop inside the daily stack:

1. Science mechanism.
2. Exercise demonstration.
3. Client-safe proof.
4. Behaviour or identity science.
5. Saveable carousel or checklist.
6. Shannon point of view, personal proof or coaching invitation.

At three posts per day, one loop takes two days. Keep all six jobs. If no public-safe client proof exists, use Shannon's own logged behaviour, anonymous aggregate proof, a coaching observation, or app workflow proof. Do not silently delete the proof slot.

## Current Campaign: The Brain That Learns To Train

The predictive-brain passion project should not stop at "your brain constructs reality." The next act is how repeated action, cues, bodily signals and social evidence teach the brain what to expect.

Campaign promise:

> Your behaviour is not a fixed personality trait. Your brain learns predictions from repeated evidence, and the right structure can help you give it better evidence.

### Published foundation

1. Free will and nonconscious action.
2. Your brain predicts reality.
3. Interoception: the body signal changed the experience.
4. Expectation sharpened visual processing.
5. Rubber-hand illusion and constructed body ownership.

### Next brain sequence

1. **Hunger is constructed.** Hunger is a real experience assembled from body signals, context, memory and expectation. Practical bridge: build meals and environments that create better evidence.
2. **Dopamine is not just pleasure.** It helps update predictions about what is worth repeating. Practical bridge: reward the start and the check-in, not only the final result.
3. **Why motivation often arrives after starting.** The predicted cost is highest before the first action. Practical bridge: the ten-minute deal and a visible first step.
4. **Your environment trains your predictions.** Repeated cues make some actions feel obvious and others unlikely. Practical bridge: prepare the next workout, meal and reminder before willpower is required.
5. **A missed session is evidence, not identity.** One miss does not define the model. Repeated recovery teaches a faster comeback.
6. **Exercise changes what effort feels like.** Fitness changes bodily feedback and the brain's expectation of cost. The work can genuinely become easier to initiate.
7. **Identity follows evidence.** "I am consistent" becomes believable after enough small receipts. Track the work and make wins visible.
8. **Other people change what feels normal.** Community supplies social evidence. This is the natural bridge to Balance accountability and coaching.

Every brain Reel must end with one action the viewer can take within 24 hours. Abstract neuroscience alone builds admiration. Applied neuroscience builds demand for coaching.

## Proof Post Rewrite Rule

Do not lead public proof with an internal report such as:

- `Week 4 complete.`
- `63 feed moments.`
- `This is Shane.`

Lead with the belief the evidence proves:

- `The people becoming consistent inside Balance are not having perfect weeks.`
- `This is what 46 ordinary workout days turns into.`
- `He did not need more motivation. He needed somewhere to keep returning to.`
- `The win was not the PB. It was checking in on the week he normally disappeared.`

Then show the evidence, Shannon's coaching interpretation and a small invitation.

Proof structure:

1. Viewer tension.
2. Specific real evidence.
3. What Shannon notices as a coach.
4. The belief this evidence supports.
5. Soft next step.


Reject or rewrite the idea if:

- it is interesting but does not connect to a viewer problem;
- it teaches without revealing anything about Shannon's coaching method;
- the proof makes the client a statistic instead of someone the viewer can recognise themselves in;
- the CTA jumps from a tiny fact to a large commitment;
- the claim is stronger than the evidence;
- the post repeats a paper, exercise or hook already in the ledger without a deliberate new angle.

## Production Standards

### Science and brain

- Use primary sources.
- Keep the claim faithful to the design and population.
- Distinguish mechanism, animal work, observation and human trials.
- Show the paper or source trail.
- Translate the finding into one useful action.
- Use the current predictive-brain visual system while that campaign is active.

### Exercise and coaching

- Use Shannon's real exercise footage where possible.
- Lead with a problem, mistake, benefit or decision.
- Show what to look for, why it matters and how to adjust it.
- Make the post saveable.
- Avoid pretending a clean demonstration is bad form.

### Proof

- Use only public-safe, approved or anonymised evidence.
- Protect faces, names, handles, locations, private messages and health details.
- Prefer a human pattern over an activity report.
- Position Balance as Shannon's coaching and support structure, not an app-only product.

### Public copy

- Casual, direct and useful.
- No corporate copy.
- No em dashes.
- No fake urgency.
- No medical promises.
- No manipulation or shame.

### Avatar production

- Create and approve the Shannon voice track in ElevenLabs first.
- Use HeyGen Video Translation to lip-sync an approved reusable Shannon source clip to that audio.
- Do not use prompt-based HeyGen video generation to create the complete Reel. Use normal HeyGen Video/avatar generation only when Video Translation cannot produce a usable result, no suitable source clip exists, quality fails, or Shannon explicitly requests fresh avatar generation.
- Build the final Reel separately using the lip-synced presenter, real footage, graphics, captions and music.

## 14-Day Three-Post Experiment

Hypothesis:

> A daily BELIEF, COACH and PROOF stack will increase qualified profile actions and coaching conversations because the viewer receives explanation, demonstration and social evidence in the same day.

Primary KPI:

- Problem-qualified, offer-ready and buyer-intent DMs attributed to content per 1,000 Instagram reach.

Diagnostics:

- Profile visits.
- Follows.
- Reel three-second retention and average watch time.
- Saves and shares.
- Story replies.
- `BALANCE` keyword DMs.
- Checkout starts and purchases with preserved attribution.

Guardrails:

- Unfollows.
- Falling reach per post across the test.
- Reel skip rate.
- Weak or repetitive posts created only to hit volume.
- Shannon production time per published unit.

Decision:

- Review after 14 complete days.
- Compare the three-post stack with the previous 14 days.
- Keep the cadence only if total qualified conversations and business outcomes rise without an unacceptable quality or production cost.

Do not judge a post from its first few hours of likes. Capture 24-hour, 72-hour and 7-day performance.
