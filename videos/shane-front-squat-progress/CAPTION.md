On 30 May, we set Shane an eight-week target: build his front squat towards 150 kg.

The plan wasn’t just “go heavier”. Heavy work. Volume and tempo. Speed and skill. Then we adjusted from the videos and the sessions he actually completed.

Shane still picked apart the first rep because that’s Shane 😂 But the better bit was what he said afterwards: it felt good, and it didn’t feel like a one-off he’d never do again.

Clear plan. Real feedback. One session at a time.
