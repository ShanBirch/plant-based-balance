I'm Shannon. I ran a personal training studio in Hampton for eight years and have experience working alongside physiotherapists and osteopaths. That experience shapes how I coach: pay attention to the person, teach the movement properly, and build from a manageable starting point.

If you're getting back into exercise, feel unsure about technique, or prefer support without a busy gym, we can work on building strong foundations at home.

A few practical questions.

Do I need a home gym?

No full home gym is required to enquire. Tell me what space and equipment you have, even if that's very little, and we'll discuss what's practical before booking.

What if I'm worried about an old injury?

Tell me before we arrange training so we can discuss whether the service is suitable and account for any guidance from your treating practitioner. Personal training is not injury diagnosis or treatment.

How much are sessions?

Message me with your suburb, goals and preferred days. I'll confirm session options, pricing and availability with you before you book.
